How to play

Attackers: fly your drone in prep and hold the scan key on defenders to identify them. Then breach in, plant the defuser at the bomb and protect it until it activates.

Defenders: reinforce soft walls, barricade doors and windows, watch the cameras, and stop the plant. If the defuser goes down, disable it before time runs out.

Soft walls can be shot through and eventually broken. Reinforced walls need Thermite. Listen for footsteps, markers show where noise comes from.

Ranked (online Quick Match) has map and operator bans, bomb site voting, and first to 4 wins. Play 5 placement matches to earn a rank, from Copper up to Champion. Bigger teams are worth more rating: 1v1 x0.6, 2v2 x0.85, 3v3 x1.1. Change any key under Controls & keybinds.
